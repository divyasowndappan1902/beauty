Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our products and services.

Are your products cruelty-free and vegan?

Yes, all Stackly Beauty products are 100% cruelty-free and vegan. We never test on animals and ensure our suppliers adhere to the same strict ethical standards.

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee. If you're not completely satisfied with your purchase, you can return gently used products within 30 days of receipt for a full refund.

How long does shipping usually take?

Standard shipping typically takes 3-5 business days within the country. Express shipping options (1-2 business days) are available at checkout.

Are your products suitable for sensitive skin?

Absolutely! Our formulations are meticulously crafted with gentle, botanically-derived ingredients and are free from harsh chemicals, sulfates, and synthetic fragrances, making them ideal for sensitive skin.
